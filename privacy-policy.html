Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Introduction

Stardrop ("we", "our", or "the app") is a social tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of the app.

Information We Collect

The app collects your email address and password when you create an account (managed securely by Clerk). The app may also access your Twitter/X handle to provide mention tracking functionality.

How We Use Your Information

Your account information is used to authenticate you and provide personalized features. Twitter data is used solely to display your mentions and related information. We do not sell or share your data for advertising purposes.

Authentication

Account authentication is handled by Clerk, a third-party authentication provider. Clerk's privacy policy governs how your authentication credentials are stored and processed.

Data Storage

Application data is stored securely on our servers. Authentication data is managed by Clerk.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Clerk for authentication and may interact with the Twitter/X API to provide mention tracking functionality.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact
